Physical relay behavior (Endpoint: relay_left)
Controls the electrical mains relay independently of the logical Zigbee On/Off state. Follow state immediately synchronizes mains to the logical state. Always on keeps the load energized and is recommended for smart bulbs/dimmers. Always off keeps mains de-energized while logical state can still change. Changing this setting can immediately switch mains power. The setting persists across restart.
